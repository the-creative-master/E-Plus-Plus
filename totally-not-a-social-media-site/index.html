    Totally Not A Social Media Site

    A parody of every platform that has ever told you to log in, and of every country that has ever answered with this page has been blocked.

    • No login. Type a name. BOOM.
    • Real uploads. Your videos are stored as blobs in the browser and catalogued by videostorage.js.
    • Streaming playback. The first ten seconds are pulled as soon as a video appears on screen; the next ten seconds arrive while you watch. Nothing lags, nothing re-downloads.
    • Exportable. Export the library and you get a real videostorage.js file with the videos inside. Drop it next to this page and the feed is back.
    • Everything is a joke. Including the ban list, which is entirely fictional.
